Sindh Desert Expedition — Cinematic Event Reel
Another cinematic project wrapped. 🎬
Another cinematic project wrapped. 🎬 Had the opportunity to create a full cinematic event reel for influencer Qasim Umer during the Sindh Desert Expedition, hosted by the only and one of the biggest offroading club "Offroad Titans" in Pakistan, truly a massive achievement and an unforgettable experience.

From the raw desert atmosphere to the offroad action and community energy, the entire project was captured with a cinematic vision and brought to life through editing, storytelling, and professional color grading.

The goal was simple: make people feel the adventure, not just watch it.

Grateful to be trusted with projects like this and excited for what's coming next.

Badgoi Top & Dasht-e-Laila 4x4 Offroad Expedition
Another milestone unlocked. 🎬🏔️
My first project with Qasim Umer was the Badgoi Top & Dasht-e-Laila 4x4 Offroad Expedition an unforgettable adventure filmed in extreme negative temperatures at one of the toughest and most challenging locations in Pakistan.

From harsh weather conditions and dangerous terrains to capturing the raw beauty of the mountains and the spirit of offroad adventure, this project pushed both creativity and endurance to another level.

Proud to share that the vlog has now crossed 252K+ views on Qasim Umer's YouTube channel.

Grateful for the trust, the experience, and the opportunity to turn real adventures into cinematic stories.
